# PART 1 — WHAT THIS PROJECT IS

## The Central Idea

This is not a website about an AI company. It is an AI Opportunity Discovery Experience. The product is the experience of discovering that someone has already mapped 104 operational intelligence systems across 12 industries and organized them by how businesses actually work — not by technology, not by department, but by operational function.

The company exists to help operational businesses in Gujarat and Western India identify, prioritize, design, and implement AI systems that create measurable business value.

The question the website answers, for every visitor: **Where should AI be implemented inside my business to create the greatest operational and financial impact?**

## What It Must Never Feel Like

Not an agency portfolio. Not a services catalog. Not a SaaS product page. Not a consulting firm's brochure. Not an AI company that explains AI. Not a startup that performs innovation. The moment it reads like any of those, it has failed.

## The Emotional Territory

The intersection of authority and wonder. Authority without wonder becomes a corporate briefing. Wonder without authority becomes entertainment. The project requires both simultaneously.

The base emotional register is **premium stillness**. Not excitement. Not motion. Stillness that communicates everything is prepared, organized, and waiting. The action — the discovery, the scramble, the silence, the revelation — interrupts this stillness and returns to it. The stillness is the brand. The discoveries are the evidence.

## The Single Memory

If a visitor remembers only one thing: **"They've already mapped my business."**

Not "they have an AI product." Not "they understand technology." The memory is of prior knowledge — that before the visitor arrived, someone had already done the work of understanding where AI applies inside businesses like theirs.

# PART 3 — BRAND FOUNDATION

## What the Company Actually Does

Helps operational businesses identify where AI should be implemented — not through technology education, but through operational understanding. The starting point is always the business problem, never the technology.

Most AI companies ask: "What can AI do?" We ask: "What business problem is worth solving?" Then: "What is the best implementation path?"

## Brand Archetypes

**Primary: Sage** — explains, reveals, teaches, clarifies, diagnoses. Authority through understanding, not credential.

**Secondary: Ruler** — creates structure, order, control, visibility, confidence.

## Brand Personality

The company feels: intelligent, experienced, practical, calm, precise, disciplined, trustworthy, outcome-focused, engineered, professional.

The company never feels: gimmicky, hype-driven, trend-chasing, exaggerated, chaotic, flashy for its own sake.

## The Classified Register

All copy must pass this test: **Any copy that a mid-2010s SaaS company would feel comfortable using is wrong.**

The register is:
- Precise (specific numbers over approximations)
- Operational (business outcomes over technology features)
- Direct (active verbs over passive constructions)
- Clinical (declarative over enthusiastic)
- Confident (no hedging: not "can help," not "may improve")

The register is not:
- Clever (no wordplay, no metaphors in metadata)
- Warm (no "we'd love to help you")
- Technocratic (no "leveraging," "solutions," "ecosystem," "empower")
- Conversational (no "So here's the thing…")

Banned vocabulary: "AI-powered," "leverage AI," "AI-driven solutions," "unlock your potential," "seamless," "robust," "cutting-edge."

## Non-Negotiable Principles

- Lead with business problems.
- Lead with operational realities.
- Connect AI to measurable outcomes.
- Demonstrate understanding before expertise.
- Demonstrate expertise before selling.
- Prioritize proof over claims.
- Prioritize trust over conversion.
- Operational reality always comes before technology.
- Specificity creates authority. Generic claims destroy authority.
- Proof is mandatory. Claims are insufficient.

# PART 4 — TRUST ARCHITECTURE

The experience builds trust in this exact sequence. Skipping layers destroys the architecture.

1. **Operational Understanding** — The company understands my reality.
2. **Industry Understanding** — The company understands my industry.
3. **Specificity** — The company understands specific details.
4. **Pattern Recognition** — The company has seen this before.
5. **Implementation Understanding** — The company knows what works.
6. **Proof** — The company has evidence.
7. **Social Validation** — Other companies have done this.

The visitor should trust the company because they understand the visitor's reality. Testimonials (Layer 7) reinforce trust that already exists. They are never the lead.

**No named testimonials at V1 launch.** The Proof page is anonymized deployment evidence only. Testimonials are reconsidered only once a critical mass of clients with explicit case-study consent exists, and even then they surface as minor, clearly secondary elements beneath deployment evidence.

# PART 5 — THE EMOTIONAL JOURNEY

The visitor moves through these stages. The experience is designed to create each stage, not skip to conversion.

1. **Recognition** — "These people understand businesses like mine."
2. **Curiosity** — "What opportunities am I missing?"
3. **Diagnostic Recognition** — "How do they already know this about my business?" (THE MOST IMPORTANT MOMENT)
4. **Possibility** — "This would actually improve how we operate."
5. **Validation** — "This already exists. This removes the risk."
6. **Strategic Desire** — The visitor mentally applies systems to their own company.
7. **Conversation Readiness** — "I need help evaluating these opportunities."

Conversion should feel like the natural next step. Not a sales action. The experience creates inevitability, not persuasion.

# PART 8 — TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM

## Typeface

**Sole typeface: Söhne by Klim Type Foundry.** No secondary typeface exists.

Söhne Mono appears only in the leader dots of the metadata stack — the fixed-width dots between label and value. That is its only use.

Söhne must be licensed before production (full weight range 300–900, italics at 300 required). Subsetting to characters used in the experience is required for performance and is permitted under the Klim license.

## The Weight Logic

Weight encodes certainty. Heavy type declares. Light type reveals. The entire emotional range of the experience is expressed through weight within one typeface.

- **Weight 900:** Architecture. The four hero words. The most permanent, most authoritative content.
- **Weight 700:** Titles. The Signal scramble title. Strong declarations.
- **Weight 600:** Outcomes. Strong emphasis within body context.
- **Weight 500:** Navigation. Labels. System metadata. Knows what it is.
- **Weight 400:** Body prose. Mechanism columns. Proof entries. The working voice.
- **Weight 300 italic:** Revelation. The reality sentence. The mechanism summary line. These two uses only. A heavy italic is a fact. A light italic is a discovery.

# PART 9 — SITE ARCHITECTURE

## Site Map (Multi-Page)

The single-page mandate from Phase 2 Specification is retired. The multi-page structure is the final architecture.

```
/ ................ Homepage
/explorer ........ Explorer overview (104 nodes, full constellation)
/explorer/[slug] . 104 individual node pages (e.g. /explorer/production-delay-prediction)
/industries ...... Industries index hub
/industries/[x] .. 10 industry pages (Manufacturing, Textile, Pharma, FMCG,
                   Distribution, Logistics, Packaging, Engineering, Chemicals, Export)
/proof ........... Evidence hub (by operational cluster)
/contact ......... Assessment request form
```

**Retired pages:** `/about` is retired. `/method` is not built at V1. `/insights` is not built at V1. These may be added later but are not part of this specification.

## Individual Node Pages (`/explorer/[node-slug]`)

These are the single highest-leverage structural change in the project. Each of the 104 nodes has a real, crawlable URL. This makes the knowledge moat indexable.

**Slug format:** kebab-case of the full workflow name. Example: `production-delay-prediction`, `sales-followup-intelligence-system`.

**Each node page:**
- Renders the full constellation in background, frozen, at 15% opacity
- Signal panel is open by default (client-side shallow routing — no page reload when navigating from within `/explorer`)
- Unique `<title>`: `[Workflow Full Name] — [Company Name]`
- Unique `<meta description>`: The reality sentence for that workflow (under 15 words, already written)
- Unique Open Graph image: a frozen still of that node's resolved panel state (must be generated for all 104 nodes before launch)
- Self-referencing canonical tag: `<link rel="canonical" href="/explorer/[node-slug]">`

**How entry works:** A visitor arriving at `/explorer/production-delay-prediction` from Google sees the constellation at 15% opacity with the Production Delay Prediction Signal panel open. The experience is identical to clicking that node from within the Explorer. The loading screen runs (0.8s, return-visitor style) before the page appears.

## Navigation

**Desktop:**
- Wordmark: fixed top-left, 14px, weight 700, letter-spacing 0.08em, ALL-CAPS
- Nav links: centered, 5 items — `EXPLORER · INDUSTRIES · PROOF · CONTACT` — wait, see note below
- CTA: `BEGIN ASSESSMENT` pill, far right

**Note:** With `/insights` and `/method` removed from V1, the nav items are: `EXPLORER · INDUSTRIES · PROOF · CONTACT`. Four items. No sub-dropdowns on the proof or contact links. `INDUSTRIES` has a flyout listing the 10 industry pages plus the `/industries` index at the top.

**Mobile:** Wordmark left (14px), hamburger right. On open: full-screen overlay at `--void`, centered nav links in 14px small-caps, 32px vertical spacing. `BEGIN ASSESSMENT` as full-width button at viewport bottom. Overlay fades in at 0.3s (no slide animation — simply appears).

## CTA Hierarchy

The CTAs get more direct as conviction increases. Each level has its own wording. Never style progression CTAs like conversion CTAs.

1. **In-panel Surface 1** — `Begin the assessment.` (headline, sentence case, weight 500, with period) / `BEGIN ASSESSMENT` (button label, ALL-CAPS)
2. **Explorer Surface 2** — `BEGIN THE ASSESSMENT` (11px small-caps, `--text-structural`) — appears after second Signal open in session
3. **Global nav CTA** — `BEGIN ASSESSMENT` (pill button, always present, every page)
4. **Progression CTAs** — `SEE ALL TEXTILE SYSTEMS`, `EXPLORE THE SYSTEMS BEHIND THIS PROOF` etc. — never styled like conversion CTAs

## Filter State and URL Logic

- Industry filter state is URL-driven only: `/explorer?industry=textile`
- Filter state is never session-persisted
- Clicking bare `EXPLORER` nav link always clears any active filter and returns to full constellation overview
- Individual node slugs are real paths, not query parameters

## Surface 2 — Entry Warmth Logic

- **Cold entry (homepage, bare /explorer):** Surface 2 appears after visitor's second Signal panel open in current session
- **Warm entry (pre-filtered industry link, direct /explorer/[node-slug] URL):** Surface 2 appears after visitor's first Signal panel open in current session

# PART 10 — THE HOMEPAGE

## One Sentence Narrative Arc

**"We already see your operations clearly enough to show you, before you've told us anything, that there's a map waiting for your business."**

Every block serves this one arc.

## Six-Block Structure

### Pre-Act: Loading Screen

**Duration:** 2.2–3.0s first visit. 0.8s returning visitor.

**Sequence:**
1. Screen at `--void` (`#050507`). Nothing. 0.15s hold.
2. Company wordmark scrambles into existence. Character pool: `A-Z, 0-9, ·, |, #, ‡, ░`. Refresh at 30fps (not 60fps — mechanical quality, not smooth). Lock direction: left-to-right, word by word. Lock timing varies ±0.08s per character for organic feel. Last character: 2 extra frames before committing. Total duration: 1.8–2.4s.
3. Metadata line assembles simultaneously with last word of wordmark, character by character, left-to-right, at 70 chars/second. Text: `INITIALIZING OPERATIONAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM`. Typography: 11px, letter-spacing 0.16em, `--text-muted`.
4. When wordmark locks: metadata line fades to opacity 0 over 0.4s.
5. Hold: 0.7s. Wordmark alone in void.
6. Dissolve: full loading screen fades to `--gallery` (`#faf9f7`) over 0.6s. Not a wipe — a fade through a brief warm luminous mid-state.

**Returning visitor:** If session flag set, duration 0.8s. Scramble at full speed. No hold. Immediate fade.

**On cold entry to non-homepage pages:** Brief loading screen (0.8s, wordmark only, no metadata line) before page content appears.

### Block 1: Gallery

**Background:** `#faf9f7` — paper white with warmth.

**Spatial description:** The four words centered vertically at 47% of viewport height (not 50% — visual center sits slightly above mathematical center). Left-aligned to content column, max-width 640px on desktop.

**The four hero words:**
```
VISIBILITY.
COORDINATION.
PREDICTION.
CONTROL.
```
118px, weight 900, tracking -0.025em, color `#0f0f0d`, line-height 0.96. Each word on its own line. Each followed by a period. The periods communicate finality — these are not words, they are declarations.

**Content does not arrive. It is present.** After the loading screen dissolves, the Gallery is simply there.

**Sub-headline:** 21px, weight 400, `--light-tertiary`, max-width 440px, line-height 1.5. 56px gap below last hero word.
```
The operational state your business is building toward.
```

**Meta line:** 11px, weight 500, letter-spacing 0.14em, `--light-muted`, centered within content column. 24px gap below sub-headline.
```

```

**Scroll indicator:** Bottom of viewport, centered. 14px chevron at `--light-ghost`. 10px small-caps label `EXPLORE THE INTELLIGENCE MAP` at `--light-muted`, 6px right of chevron. Pulses once every 3 seconds: `opacity: 0.3 → 0.8 → 0.3`.

**Nothing else in the Gallery.** No nav links. No team references. No service descriptions. Nothing competes with the four words.

### Block 2: Eclipse Transition

200vh scroll buffer. CSS `animation-timeline: scroll()` driving `@keyframes`. Linear timing throughout (`cubic-bezier(0,0,1,1)`). The linearity makes it feel like a change of fact, not a dramatic event.

**Transformation checkpoints:**

| 20% | — | rgba(10,10,15,0.20) + blur(4px) | — | — | — | beginning to shift |
| 40% | #504e4a (dusk) | rgba(10,10,15,0.60) + blur(10px) | — | — | — | — |

| 80% | #0c0c11 | full glass | opacity 0.70 | opacity 0.70 | opacity 0.08 | outline mid |
| 100% | #0a0a0f | full glass | opacity 1.00 | opacity 1.00 | opacity 0.18 | full outline |

**The dusk color (#504e4a) at 40%:** The Eclipse does not go straight from warm-white to cold-dark. It passes through a warm dusk — the color of threshold between day and operational night. The visitor feels like the lights in a control room are dimming.

**Hero text transitions:** `#0f0f0d → #b0aca4 → #f2f0ec` through the Eclipse, at the same rate as background.

**Constellation emergence:** The constellation does not animate into position. It does not build. As the darkness reaches the right depth, it becomes visible — the darkness was always a curtain. The constellation was always there. The visitor watches the darkness become transparent enough to reveal what already existed.

### Block 3: Explorer Teaser

A constrained render of the constellation component: 12–15 representative nodes from 4 clusters, no pan/zoom, no filter (clicking filter shows `VIEW FULL MAP →` prompt redirecting to `/explorer`), node clicks direct to `/explorer` with that node selected.

Identical to full Explorer in visual treatment — same star field, same kinetic lines at correct proportions, same node glow characters. Its one job: make `/explorer` feel inevitable.

The deployment counter. Proof entries in classified format. Single CTA: `ENTER THE INTELLIGENCE MAP →` in 13px small-caps, `--text-tertiary`.

### Block 6: Closing CTA

**CTA Pill:** Far right. Label: `BEGIN ASSESSMENT`, 11px, weight 600, letter-spacing 0.12em. Container: 40px height, 20px horizontal padding, border-radius 20px (full pill).

## Background Behavior

**Gallery state:** Fully transparent. The navbar floats above `#faf9f7` with no background.

**Eclipse (20%–60% progress):** Backdrop begins: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px) saturate(1.4)` with `background: --nav-glass`. Transition starts at 20% Eclipse progress, completes at 60%.

**Explorer / all dark pages:** Glass backdrop permanent: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px) saturate(1.4); background: rgba(10,10,15,0.82)`.

**Scroll-back (returning up through Eclipse):** Glass dissolves proportionally — responsive to scroll position, not one-way trigger.

# PART 13 — THE SIGNAL

## What the Signal Is

A dossier. A file compiled about a specific operational intelligence, waiting to be accessed. Not a panel. Not a product page. The "CLOSE FILE" exit trigger names it: files exist in this system. Files are closed when the access event ends.

## Node Click Sequence

**0.00s:** Click registered. Node emits radial pulse: `width: 0 → 48px, opacity: 0.6 → 0` over 0.35s. This is the only moment anything expands outward in the entire experience. Everything else contracts, focuses, or arrives.

**0.20s:** Kinetic lines begin decelerating: each reaches zero at slightly different time (±0.05s variation) — organic wind-down, not synchronized stop.

**0.40s:** All motion stopped. Everything frozen. Star field and ghost text remain (they never move). Node pulses suspended. 

**0.40–0.80s:** 0.4s hold. System is focusing. Visitor waits. Second silence (before the primary one inside the panel).

**0.80s:** Signal panel arrives.

## Panel Entry

**Not a slide.** A commit.

`transform: translateX(8px) scaleX(0.98)` and `opacity: 0` → `transform: translateX(0) scaleX(1)` and `opacity: 1` over 0.45s with `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`. Extreme ease-out. A door swinging to its natural stop. The 8px translate and 0.98 scale are subliminal — the visitor feels weight, not movement.

## Panel Dimensions and Surface

**Desktop:** Full viewport height. 88vw width. Background: `#141418`.

**Left edge:** 1px line at `rgba(242,238,228,0.06)`. The thinnest possible separator between two planes of operational darkness.

**The 12vw Explorer sliver:** The constellation remains visible in the 12vw left sliver at 15% opacity — frozen, present. The visitor has not left the constellation. They have gone deeper into it.

**Panel background gradient:** `radial-gradient(ellipse 120% 80% at 50% 0%, rgba(26,26,32,0.8) 0%, transparent 60%), #141418`. A faint brightening at the panel top — as if the file's heading receives more light than its contents. ~5-7% brightness differential. Invisible as an effect, felt as warmth.

**Content zone:** Left margin 48px. Max-width 600px.

## Signal Sequence (Final — Supersedes All Prior Sequences)

**The full sequence from click to close:**

1. Node click → radial pulse (0.35s) → kinetic lines decelerate (0.40s) → full freeze hold (0.40s) → panel commits (0.45s)
2. Orientation line: **present when panel arrives.** Does not stagger in.
3. Scramble assembly (2.0–3.5s depending on title length)
4. Metadata stack stagger (0.96s total)
5. **SILENCE: `SIGNAL_SILENCE_MS = 900`**
6. Reality sentence: opacity 0 → 1 over 0.6s. No translate. No scale. Becomes present.
7. 1.5s hold → chevron pulse (once). Pulses once more if visitor idle 8s.
8. Panel body scrollable: **Problem → Outcomes → Proof → Mechanism → CTA**
9. CTA block: `Begin the assessment.` + subline + `BEGIN ASSESSMENT` button
10. `CLOSE FILE` below button
11. Panel close → Explorer resumes

**The Signal sequence is different from Phase 2 Specification in two ways:**
- **Section sequence:** Changed to Problem → Outcomes → **Proof → Mechanism** (not Mechanism → Proof). Proof appears before Mechanism because skeptical visitors leave before reaching the most attention-demanding content. Proof reduces skepticism before Mechanism demands understanding.
- **CTA position:** After Proof, before CLOSE FILE (not after Mechanism).

## The Scramble

**Character refresh:** 30fps. Not 60fps. Lower rate = characters read as glyphs before changing = mechanical quality, not smooth.

**Primary pool:** `ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ`. Secondary pool (15% frequency): `0123456789·|#‡░▓`. The secondary pool makes the scramble look like it's working through possibilities, not displaying random noise.

**Lock sequence:** Left-to-right within each word. Each character's lock timing varies ±0.08s from scheduled time. Some lock slightly early, some late. The irregularity makes assembly feel like a system resolving uncertainty.

**Character color during scramble:** `--text-muted` (`#565450`). At lock: shifts to `--text-primary` (`#f2f0ec`) in 0.08s. The color shift at each lock is the moment of resolution — uncertain to certain.

**Between words:** 0.3s silence. No scrambling. The completed word hangs at full opacity. The visitor reads it.

**Single-line (≤28 characters):** 64px, weight 700, ALL-CAPS, tracking 0.04em, `--text-primary`. Duration 2.0–2.5s.

**Two-line (>28 characters):** 52px, weight 700. First line scrambles and locks. 0.15s gap. Second line begins. Duration 2.5–3.5s total. The orientation line and metadata stack do not begin until both lines are fully assembled.

## The Orientation Line

Appears immediately when scramble completes. Already visible — does not stagger.

Format: `4 of 11 SYSTEMS · PRODUCTION INTELLIGENCE`

- Numbers and separators: 10px, weight 500, small-caps, `#5a5856`
- Cluster name: slightly brighter, `#6a6864`
- Tracking: 0.10em

This gives the visitor their coordinate in the operational landscape. More important on mobile (where the constellation behind the panel is hidden) than on desktop.

## The Metadata Stack

Begins after scramble completes. 16px below the orientation line.

**Stagger timing:**
- Line 1 (WATCHES): `opacity: 0, translateY: 6px → 1, 0` over 0.3s. Delay: 0s.
- Line 2 (GENERATES): same. Delay: 0.22s.
- Line 3 (REPLACES/TRANSFORMS): same. Delay: 0.44s.
- Line 4 (RETURNS): same. Delay: 0.66s.

Lines overlap slightly (line 2 begins before line 1 fully completes). Total stack assembly: 0.96s.

**Typography:** 11px, weight 400, ALL-CAPS, tracking 0.12em.

**Label:** `--text-tertiary` (`#8a8880`). Leader dots: `--text-ghost` (`#3a3836`), Söhne Mono for fixed-width alignment. Value: `--text-secondary` (`#c8c4bc`).

Label column: 160px total width. Values begin at consistent horizontal position.

**WATCHES** — what data the system consumes (literal, specific)
**GENERATES** — what the system produces, with timing where applicable
**REPLACES** — the manual or reactive activity made unnecessary. For augmentation systems (visibility tools, dashboards) where nothing is replaced: this becomes **TRANSFORMS** — the shift from displacement to transformation is semantically honest. Apply TRANSFORMS for: all Management Synthesis systems, all visibility dashboards, all reporting automation.
**RETURNS** — the business outcome as a measurable condition, not a feature claim.

## The Silence

```javascript
const SIGNAL_SILENCE_MS = 900;
// DO NOT REDUCE.
// This 0.9-second pause is a protected design decision.
// It is the moment the visitor processes what they have read.
// It is not a loading delay. It is a held beat.
// Shortening it requires written approval from the design lead.
```

0.9 seconds. Nothing happens. Not even the chevron. The panel holds.

This is where understanding occurs. The visitor's memory of their own operational failures activates. The reality sentence arrives into a primed state.

**Returning visitor silence:** 0.3s (reduced but not eliminated — the moment of understanding is abbreviated, not removed).

**Reduced motion exception:** The silence is not an animation. It is preserved in full even when `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is active.

## The Reality Sentence

**Position:** Visual center of the panel's remaining space below the metadata stack. Not top-edge. Center.

**Arrival:** `opacity: 0 → 1` over 0.6s. No translate. No scale. It simply becomes present, as if it was always there and the darkness was concealing it.

**Typography:** 28px, weight 300, italic, line-height 1.55, max-width 500px, left-aligned to content margin. Color: `--reality-text` (`#ede8e0`) — the warmest, softest text in the panel. Softer because it is the most human.

**The weight 300 italic is the most important typographic decision in the specification.** The title arrived at 700. The metadata at 400. The reality sentence arrives lighter than everything before it. Weight communicates certainty. The reality sentence does not declare. It reveals.

**After 1.5s:** Single downward chevron at panel bottom, `--text-ghost`, pulses once: `opacity: 0.3 → 0.7 → 0.3` over 1.5s. Once only. If visitor idle 8s, pulses once more.

# PART 15 — CONTENT RULES

## The Reality Sentence

This is the most important writing task in the project. Written 104 times. Not templated.

**Rules:**
1. One sentence
2. Under 15 words (14 or fewer; 15 is the absolute maximum)
3. Names the operational failure in its most precise form
4. Written in present tense — the failure is happening now
5. Does not mention AI
6. Does not mention technology
7. Addresses the person who feels this failure most acutely (the person who lives the gap, not the person who manages the system)
8. Uses the specific vocabulary of that reader's operational context

**The test for every reality sentence:** Can a 52-year-old founder from Surat read this sentence and feel — *that is my problem, stated in a way I would never have thought to state it* — rather than — *that is a description of a problem I have heard described before?*

**Prerequisite:** Before writing a single reality sentence, build the taxonomy of readers against workflows. For each workflow, identify the primary reader (not "Manufacturing" — specifically "Plant Manager at a 150-person auto component supplier"), name the vocabulary that reader uses to describe the failure, then write the sentence in that vocabulary.

The Production Delay Prediction sentence is not the Machine Downtime Warning sentence — even though both are manufacturing, both predictive, both operational failure. Different readers. Different vocabulary. The sentence must know the difference.

## The Metadata Stack Template

```
WATCHES    ·············  [2–5 specific data inputs, noun phrases, separated by ·]
GENERATES  ·············  [primary AI output] · [delivery timing if applicable]
REPLACES   ·············  [specific manual activity or reactive process displaced]
RETURNS    ·············  [business outcome as measurable condition, not feature]
```

**REPLACES → TRANSFORMS substitution:** If the visitor would say "we didn't really have a way to do this before," use TRANSFORMS. If they can describe what they used to do manually, use REPLACES.

Apply TRANSFORMS to: all Management Synthesis systems, all visibility dashboards, all reporting automation systems.

## The Problem Paragraph

3–4 sentences. Second person. Past tense for failure state, present tense for underlying cause. The paragraph should make the visitor feel slightly seen — as if the writer has spent time inside their building.

No sentence may begin with "AI." No sentence may end with "using AI." No sentence may contain "leverage AI." The paragraph is about the business problem. Technology appears only in the mechanism section.

## The Proof Section Rules

- Anonymized to industry category, not company name
- Geographically specific (state, not city — "Gujarat" not "Surat")
- Temporally anchored (`operational since 2023` — a year, not a quarter)
- Industry-accurate — do not use a pharma proof reference for a manufacturing workflow
- The counter (`DEPLOYED ACROSS N INDUSTRY CATEGORIES`) must be literally accurate
- Every proof line must trace to an actual completed deployment in internal records

## CMS Field Structure

Every workflow in the database requires:
- `node_id` — unique identifier
- `short_name` — under 28 characters (for node hover labels, index view)
- `full_name` — no limit (for Signal scramble title)
- `reality_sentence` — under 15 words (also serves as page meta description)
- `cluster_primary` — from the 8-cluster taxonomy
- `cluster_secondary` — if dual-membership
- `industry_primary` — from the 12-industry list
- `industry_secondary` — if dual-membership
- `priority` — 1–10
- `x`, `y` — canvas coordinates (from the node layout spreadsheet)
- `dual_membership` — boolean
- `metadata_watches`, `metadata_generates`, `metadata_replaces_or_transforms`, `metadata_returns`
- `problem_paragraph` — 3–4 sentences
- `outcomes` — array of outcome strings (first with number, rest without)
- `proof_entries` — array (3–5 deployment lines in classified format)
- `proof_counter` — count of distinct industry categories in proof entries
- `mechanism_ai_handles` — array (max 5 items)
- `mechanism_you_handle` — array (max 5 items)
- `mechanism_summary` — one sentence, italic, the AI/human relationship summary

# PART 17 — CONVERSION SYSTEM

## Two Surfaces

**Surface 1 — In-Signal CTA:** Inside every Signal panel, after the Proof section, before CLOSE FILE. The visitor is at maximum conviction. The CTA appears once per Signal — does not repeat within the same Signal.

**Surface 2 — Explorer Persistent Element:** `BEGIN THE ASSESSMENT` in 11px small-caps, `--text-structural`. Positioned top-right of Explorer viewport (outside constellation area). Appears after behavior trigger (see below). Does not pulse, animate, or demand attention — simply present.

**Activation threshold:**
- Cold entry (homepage, bare `/explorer`): after second Signal panel open in current session
- Warm entry (pre-filtered industry link, direct `/explorer/[node-slug]`): after first Signal panel open

**Global nav CTA:** Always present, every page. `BEGIN ASSESSMENT` pill. The persistent fallback for the visitor who has already decided.

## The Contact Form

Specified in full in Part 14 (/contact).

## What Beliefs Must Exist Before CTA

All four of these must be true before the in-panel CTA appears:
1. This specific system addresses a real problem that exists in my business right now
2. The outcomes are specific and achievable
3. The mechanism is clear
4. It is already deployed and working in my industry sector

The CTA placement (after Proof, before Mechanism) is the implementation of conditions 1–3. Condition 4 is satisfied by the Proof section immediately above the CTA.

Wait — this is a discrepancy: the sequence is Problem → Outcomes → Proof → Mechanism → CTA. The CTA comes after Mechanism, not after Proof. The Mechanism is the last content section before the CTA. All four conditions are met by the time the visitor reaches the CTA.

**Corrected sequence: Problem → Outcomes → Proof → Mechanism → CTA → CLOSE FILE.**

This is the final sequence. The CTA appears after the visitor has experienced the complete trust architecture.

# PART 18 — IMPLEMENTATION ARCHITECTURE

## Rendering Strategy

**SVG layer:** All 104 nodes (each an accessible, ARIA-labeled, keyboard-navigable element). Thread lines connecting co-implementing nodes. Interactive elements only.

**Canvas layer:** Kinetic background lines. High-frequency redraw (60fps target). Non-interactive. No DOM composition. Sits below the SVG layer.

**HTML layer:** Signal panel, navbar, industry filter, all UI elements above the constellation.

Three distinct rendering surfaces. They must remain separate. Do not compose kinetic lines as SVG elements — they will not achieve 60fps at this line count on mid-range devices.

## Technology Stack

**Framework:** React with TypeScript.

**Constellation pan/zoom:** `d3-zoom` applied to the SVG layer.

**Eclipse transition:** CSS Scroll-Driven Animations API (`animation-timeline: scroll()`) for primary implementation. JavaScript `IntersectionObserver` + `scroll` event fallback for Firefox. The fallback must be invisible — not a degraded experience.

**Session tracking:** `sessionStorage` for: first-visit flag, visited node IDs, panel open count. `localStorage` is not used.

**Contact form:** No third-party form library. No `<form>` element. Custom `fetch` POST to serverless function.

**Spline/3D:** Not used. The constellation is 2D SVG. The atmosphere is CSS and Canvas. No 3D perspective geometry anywhere in the experience. The orthographic top-down view (the God's-Eye View) is the correct projection — 3D would add decorative complexity without operational meaning.

## Performance Requirements

- Lighthouse Performance: 90+ on 3G simulation
- Canvas kinetic lines: 60fps on mid-range Android. Implement frame-budget check — if frame time exceeds 16ms, reduce active line count.
- All 104 workflow objects loaded on initial page load: total data size under 80KB compressed. No lazy loading per workflow (would create Signal latency).
- Söhne font files: subset to characters used. Full subsetting rights under Klim license.
- SVG constellation: 104 SVG elements maximum (one per node) — do not fragment.
- Star field: generated once at mount, cached. Not regenerated on re-render.

## Accessibility

- All nodes: `role="button"`, `tabindex="0"`, `aria-label` with full workflow name
- Signal panel: `aria-modal="true"`, focus trapped on open, specific tab order: orientation line → metadata → reality sentence → scroll through body → CTA button → CLOSE FILE
- `Escape` key closes Signal panel
- Industry filter: `aria-pressed` state, arrow key navigation between items
- Keyboard navigation through constellation: Tab moves between nodes, Enter/Space opens Signal
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`: scrambles reduce to 0.3s fade-in. Silence (`SIGNAL_SILENCE_MS`) is preserved — it is not an animation.

## Protected Decisions — These Will Come Under Pressure

**1. The 0.9-second silence.** Someone will call it "broken" in testing. Someone will call it "too slow" in a demo. Someone will shorten it in a PR to "500ms" because it "feels more reasonable." Name it in the code. Document its rationale. Any PR that reduces it requires written approval from the design lead referencing this document.

**2. The classified register.** Every contributor who hasn't read this document will write SaaS copy. The register must be enforced at review time. Build a content review checklist from Part 15's register rules before content production begins.

**3. No navigation in the Gallery.** Someone will ask for a Services link. The answer: the Explorer is the services. The constellation is the about. If this case cannot be made at review time, re-read Part 10.

**4. WhatsApp only.** Someone will want an email field. See Part 14 (/contact). The WhatsApp field is not aesthetic — it is a conversion strategy decision based on how the target visitor actually communicates.

# PART 20 — PRINCIPLES THAT GOVERN ALL FUTURE DECISIONS

When a new decision arises that is not covered by this document, apply these tests in order:

**Test 1 — The Inevitability Test**
Does this make the experience more impressive, or more inevitable? Choose inevitable. A creative website tries to impress. This experience reveals something that was already true.

**Test 2 — The Register Test**
Would a mid-2010s SaaS company feel comfortable using this copy, this visual, this interaction? If yes, it is wrong for this project.

**Test 3 — The Stillness Test**
Does this add motion to something that should remain still? Typography is authority. Authority stays still. Systems move around it.

**Test 4 — The Recovery Test**
After this moment of complexity, activity, or spectacle — does the experience return to premium stillness? If not, add the return.

**Test 5 — The Memory Test**
What will the visitor remember from this? If the answer is not "they've already mapped my business," or "they named my problem exactly," or "that map was enormous and organized," reconsider.

**Test 6 — The Final Principle from Project Memory**
Every future decision should reinforce: **"These people understand where AI should be applied inside my business better than anyone else."**

If a decision strengthens that belief, it is probably correct. If it weakens it, it is probably wrong.
